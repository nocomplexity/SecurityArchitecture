# Open and Free Security Books

This section provides a curated, opinionated list of high-quality cyber security books. Where possible, titles are available as PDFs and/or in an easy-to-read online format that works in any browser, without special software or required registration.

All listed books comply with the [Open Definition](http://opendefinition.org/od/2.1/en/). This means each book is:

- **Free to access** – no paywalls or registration required  
- **Free to use** – for personal, educational, or professional purposes  
- **Free to modify and share** – under the terms of their respective open licences

If you know of a great cyber security book that deserves a place in this list, please raise an [issue](https://github.com/nocomplexity/SecurityArchitecture). Contributions and suggestions are always welcome!
